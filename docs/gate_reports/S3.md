# The panel validates itself

Three pairs, one shared M1 code path (`comparators.compute_premium is
premium.compute_premium` is asserted), three distinct signatures:

| Pair | Proposed regime | n | Mean π | Last |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SKHY / 000660** | `one_way_constrained` (quota exhausted) | 12 | **+27.97%** | 22.57% |
| **TSM / 2330** | `one_way_constrained` | 2,328 | **+8.88%** | 11.20% |
| **BABA / 9988** | `fungible` (control) | 1,593 | **−0.17%** | 0.81% |

**BABA sitting at parity is the result that makes the other two believable.** A freely
convertible pair has no barrier and therefore no premium, and the identical construction
that reports +28% for SKHY reports −0.17% for BABA. That is the panel's internal validity
check, and it is the strongest evidence in the repo that the measurement is sound rather
than merely consistent.

Two second-order points worth carrying into any write-up:

- **TSM's premium is wider now than a decade ago** (recent range up to 33%, early years
  ~3–5%). Persistence is the null under a one-sided barrier; even the *direction* of drift
  is not obviously toward compression.
- **BABA's FX leg is near-constant by policy** — the HKD peg holds 7.75–7.85 under the
  HKMA Convertibility Undertaking. That is a design asset, not trivia: BABA's premium
  isolates cross-listing dynamics from FX dynamics almost completely, making it the
  cleanest available read on barrier behaviour itself.

## ⚠️ India — the regime hole

Both Indian local legs fail on every route: the keyed vendor 404s `INFY.NSE`, `.NS`,
`.BSE`, `.IN`, `ICICIBANK.NS` and `500209.BSE`, and Yahoo remains throttled. The ADR legs
and USDINR are fine.

The cost is structural, not cosmetic. The Indian pairs are the panel's **only**
`two_way_headroom` regime — bounded but non-zero conversion capacity, sitting between
TSMC's exhausted quota and BABA's full fungibility. Without them the taxonomy is a
three-point scale with its middle missing, precisely where SKHY would move if headroom
ever reopened.

**Unblocker:** one support email. Re-probed 2026-07-29 after the tier upgrade with the
cache bypassed: `INFY.NSE`, `INFY.BSE`, `INFY.IN`, `ICICIBANK.NSE` and `RELIANCE.NSE` all
return `404 Ticker Not Found`, and neither NSE nor BSE appears among the 70 exchanges the
tier exposes. `RELIANCE.NSE` failing rules out a symbology problem. See
`docs/data_sources.md`.

# Built

**`pipeline/panel/fx_conventions.py`** (25 tests) — every quote direction declared once as
**local units per USD**. The tests pin three properties beyond correctness: a reciprocal
is rejected per currency against a hand-checked observation; the error **names the fix**
("needs inverting in its adapter") rather than "out of range"; and **no range admits its
own reciprocal**, with KRW (~1450) and HKD (~7.8) asserted disjoint.

The asymmetry is why this is data, not convention: inverting FX yields ≈ −99.99% and
anyone catches it, but one reversed currency in a five-pair panel corrupts one pair while
the rest look fine — the panel then reads "mostly working" rather than broken.

**`pipeline/ingest/d5_headroom.py`** — the barrier-state observable, both programmes.

**Theme additions:** `small_multiples`, `multiples_headline`, `thin_date_ticks`. These
exist because the obvious construction fails on render in two ways — `suptitle` collides
with a `fig.text` subtitle unless the rect is reserved, and panels of very different
sample density (12 points beside 2,328) overprint their date labels.

**Two bugs fixed:** the fragility layer retried **4xx** six times with backoff (a 404 is a
statement about the *request* and burns throttle budget a real rate limit needs); and
sequenced same-day partitions would resolve backwards under full-path sorting, because
`.` sorts before `/`.

**Tests: 232 passing. All recorded raw files verify** (d1 5/5, d5 2/2, d6 9/9, events 1/1,
snapshots 1/1).
