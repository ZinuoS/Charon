issuance cap, which is a stronger citation than any press source and is now the repo's
citation of record for the mechanism.

## Two exclusions, both on documented corporate actions

Recorded because an exclusion made for the wrong reason is indistinguishable from one made
for the right reason unless the reason is written down.

**AUO — excluded entirely.** Filed Form 25 on 2019-09-20 to delist its ADSs from the NYSE,
moving to an OTC programme (AUOTY). A premium built on a thin OTC quote measures quote
staleness as much as mispricing. The 2002–2019 window is real and stays in the registry; it
is out of the pool because a delisted programme is no longer the object of study.

**ASE — restricted to 2018-05-02 onward.** The ratio check caught this, and it is the reason
that check exists. Stated ratio 2.0, implied 3.75, and a constructed premium reaching
**+57,714%**. Cause: the provider's 3711.TW series splices the predecessor 2311.TW history in
unadjusted. On 2018-04-30 the local close steps 44.5 → 80.3 while the implied ratio steps
4.0 → 2.0. **A ratio spline would not fix it** — the price step is 1.80× against a 2.0× ratio
change, so splining injects a spurious ~10% level shift. Before the exchange the two legs are
different securities.

*On the ratio check's limits:* implied/stated ≈ 1 + median π **by construction**, so it
catches order-of-magnitude errors and nothing subtler. It is a smoke alarm, not a proof, and
it is never used to tune a ratio toward a nicer-looking premium.

## An econometric correction the cohort forced

Pooling raw **levels** across pairs with different long-run means lets a constant offset —
perfectly autocorrelated at every horizon — masquerade as persistence. The more pairs you
add, the more persistent the pooled premium looks, regardless of its dynamics.

`estimate_regime` now applies a within transformation (per-pair demeaning) before stacking.
Measured effect here: ρ₁ 0.9816 raw → 0.9798 demeaned. **Small, because the four means are
close — but its size is a property of this panel, not of the estimator**, so the correction
is not optional. A test pins it with two pure white-noise series around different means,
which must not pool to high ρ.

## Why not India, which was the better candidate on paper

India's "limited two-way fungibility" is the canonical headroom regime and comes with matched
controls in the literature (Brazil, Mexico) and a within-name natural experiment. Two
independent findings ruled it out, and the second is the more interesting:

1. **Data.** Every NSE/BSE symbol returns **404** on this entitlement — `INFY.NSE`,
   `ICICIBANK.NSE`, `HDFCBANK.NSE`, `DRREDDY.NSE`, `INFY.BSE`. A 404 is a statement about the
   request, not about server load, so it is an entitlement wall rather than a throttle. The
   Yahoo route was simultaneously returning 429 (transient, and partly self-inflicted by the
   intraday tracker holding that host's budget).
2. **Regime.** The headroom rule **was repealed effective 15 December 2014** — DR Scheme 2014
   para 11(1) repealed the 1993 Scheme, and no successor instrument (NDI Rules 2019 Sch. IX,
   RBI MD 11/2017-18 Annex 8, SEBI Master Circular Ch.1 §22) reimposes a prior-cancellation
   condition. The modern constraint is a sectoral FEMA cap plus an issuer-driven procedural
   gate. So the post-2014 Indian series is **not the regime this study is about**, and
   Infosys currently trades at +0.2% — parity.

Had only the data blocked, India would still be the right target and the fix would be an
entitlement. Because the *regime* changed too, the honest target is India **pre-2014**, which
is a different and harder sourcing problem.

## The limitation, stated plainly

**These four pairs share a regulator.** Four pairs cut *issuer*-idiosyncratic noise; they give
no independent variation in the *rule*. The pooled estimate is not four independent draws from
"barrier-constrained regimes" — it is four issuers under one regime. That sentence now sits in
`REGIME_OF_PAIR`, in the notebook, and in the client note's pending table, because it is the
kind of caveat that evaporates if it lives in only one place.
