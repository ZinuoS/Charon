## 0. The finding that reframed the session

The session prompt called TSMC "the only two-decade history of this regime family". On disk
it was **ten years** — 2,328 joined sessions from 2016-07-28. The premise was checked before
anything was built on it, and the gap turned out to be recoverable.

The cause was **provider ordering, not entitlement and not the response cache.** The two
legs' chains were `("nasdaq", "yahoo_finance")` and `("twse", "yahoo_finance")`. That ordering
is deliberate and generally right — the listing venue outranks an aggregator on provenance —
but Nasdaq's API serves a rolling ten-year window and the TWSE route starts 2010. EODHD,
which the repo already pays for and which every *other* Taiwan and Brazil leg already
prefers, serves `TSM.US` from 1997-10-08 and `2330.TW` from 1994-09-05. The TSMC tier was
simply the first thing built, before EODHD existed in the repo, and was never revisited.

**Blast radius, probed rather than assumed:** TSMC only. Every other comparator already routes
EODHD-first and is deep. The three remaining `nasdaq`-first ADR legs (INFY, IBN, BABA) are
truncated too, but INFY/IBN have no local leg at all (India, ruled out twice) and BABA's join
is bounded by its 2019 HK secondary listing, so no other pair's premium is affected.

**Admitted on evidence, not preference.** EODHD's raw closes agree with the listing venues
**bit-for-bit** over the entire overlap — 2,513 shared dates on the ADR leg and 4,060 on the
local leg, `max_rel_diff = 0.00e+00`, zero mismatches. That agreement is now
`tests/test_lab_tsmc.py::test_deep_history_agrees_with_the_listing_venue_over_the_whole_overlap`,
so if it ever stops holding the ordering must be reverted rather than the tolerance loosened.

**And then most of the new history was thrown away.** Both legs are raw closes against a
constant 5.0 ratio, and TSMC paid annual stock dividends into the early 2000s. Where the two
providers adjust those inconsistently the error *accumulates into the level*: the constructed
premium averages −55% in 1997 and walks to roughly zero by 2005. Eleven one-leg-only jumps
above 5σ are detectable, all between 1997-10-09 and 2002-07-25, clustered in the June–August
ex-dividend season, **none in the following 24 years**. Adjusted closes on both legs are
worse (the legs' cash-dividend adjustments differ).

Cause-based cut: 2002-07-26. Registry cut: **2005-01-03**, the more conservative one.
Sensitivity reported in notebook 09 §1.0a: the wider sample gives **36.3%** against the
restricted sample's **41.8%** — the exclusion makes the result *less* favourable, which is the
only real answer to a curation objection.

**Net: 2,328 → 5,064 sessions. 10.0 → 21.6 years.** The premise now holds, honestly.

The `--no-cache` / `--new-partition` / `--only` flags existed on `d1_prices` alone. They now
live in `_puller.py` and every puller inherits them.

## 1. The lab's numbers

### Episode census (G25) — base rule 5pp / 10 sessions

| | |
|---|---|
| episodes in 21.6 years | **137** (71 compression, 66 widening) |
| median size / duration | **7.97pp** over **20** sessions |
| largest | 21.2pp |
| compressions closed by the ADR leg | **56.3%** (local leg 43.7%, FX 0%) |
| widenings driven by the ADR leg | **74.2%** |

The decomposition is an identity, not a regression — residual `< 1e-9` by test. **The ADR leg
is where both the convergence and the risk live**, which promotes the short-ADR expression.

The full grid also exposes something a single cell would hide: the reported episode count is
**not monotone** in the reversal threshold (355 at 3pp against 346 at 2pp, min 5 days). That
is the two-part rule behaving correctly — a larger threshold gives fewer but longer swings, so
fewer are dropped by the duration filter. The test pins monotonicity on the *raw* swing count,
which is the invariant that actually exists.

### Entry outcomes (G26) — the headline

90th-percentile entry (expanding, 504-session warmup), held 252 sessions, n=822:

| bracket | carry | beats carry | median net |
|---|---|---|---|
| low | 250bp/yr | **55.5%** | +1.27pp |
| mid | 600bp/yr | **41.8%** | −2.23pp |
| high | 1200bp/yr | **16.4%** | −8.23pp |

At the 99th percentile / 252d: **78.4% / 60.4% / 27.9%**.

Two readings. The signal is real and monotone in extremity — 60.4% at the 99th against 34.8%
at the 80th. But **the cost bracket, not the signal, decides the trade**, and four of five
carry components are still bracketed assumptions. That is the quantitative form of what the
pitch already sells: the desk conversation matters more than the view.

### Excursions and stops (G26b)

| | |
|---|---|
| median adverse excursion before resolution | **11.2pp** |
| 95th percentile | 21.7pp |
| **worst in 21.6 years** | **25.3pp** |
| **SKHY, realised, first 3 sessions** | **35.6pp** |

SKHY's first week exceeded the worst 252-day excursion in the entire comparator history — in a
pair whose barrier is *harder*. A 10pp stop fires on 54% of historical entries (22% of them
winners); a 4pp stop fires on 86%. **No stop distance bounds loss without cutting winners**,
which is the empirical case for sizing as the risk control. G24 reached the same conclusion
from the exit tree; this reaches it independently from the path distribution.

### FX (G27)

Empirical coefficient **0.856 (95% CI 0.653–1.058)** on 5,063 days, R² **1.34%**.

- On 2,327 days the interval was 0.507–1.103 and comfortably contained 1.0. On the deep
  sample the pair's own analytic coefficient (1.062) sits **just outside** it, so "theory,
  unrejected" was a statement about a short sample.
- **Not stable across eras**: 2016–2020 gives 0.31 with an interval containing zero;
  2021–2026 gives 1.26. No single hedge ratio is right for all regimes.
- The premium-as-currency-notional residual is arithmetic — `π/(1+π)` in any pair — but the
  magnitude is not. TSMC's median is 4.0% and its 21-year maximum 24.7%; **SKHY today is
  18.4%**, above this pair's 95th percentile.
- **Circularity disclosed:** the coefficient shipped on the SKHY hedge menu was estimated on
  *this* pair. It is a Taiwanese estimate applied to a Korean pair, and the managed TWD makes
  it a lower bound on the won's. `FX_COEF_EMPIRICAL` is now pinned to its own estimator by
  test, so the cached constant cannot drift from the regression it caches.

## 2. Two defects found while wiring the lab

**Cross-leg date mixing in the live snapshot.** `HedgeLegs` was built from each leg's *own*
last close. On 2026-07-29 the ADR and local legs had settled and the FX fix had not, so the
hedge panel showed a premium of **32.1%** against **22.6%** on the last shared date. A premium
is a price ratio; all three prices must be from one instant. `latest_common_legs()` now owns
the join and `HedgeLegs.live()` is the only constructor the panels use. Corrected panel
figures: residual share 24.3% → **18.4%**, residual per ADR USD 30.79 → **23.97**, FX
sensitivity 1.063pp → **0.987pp** per 1% KRW.

**Hardcoded counts and thresholds in captions.** `2,328 days` appeared in four live captions
and `~79bp/mo` in the chain figure — all stale the moment the comparator deepened. The live
ones now derive (`len(pi_tsm)`, `critical_carry_bp()/12`). Gate reports S2/S3/S4/S16/S17/S18
are left alone: they record what was true when they were written.

**Consequence of the re-fit:** pooled `one_way_constrained` half-life **310d (floor 217,
support `interpolated`)** on 2.2× the data; critical carry **963bp/yr = 80bp/mo** (was 954/79).

## 3. The visual system change

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | **BLOCKED — hexes not supplied.** Built everything else; the swap is one edit to `SEMANTIC` plus one re-render. |
| **Type** | LANDED. `Arial → Helvetica → Liberation Sans → DejaVu Sans`, with the unresolvable name filtered out of the live stack and kept in `PORTABLE_FONT_STACK`. |
| **Sizes** | Retuned once: title 13.5→15.0, subtitle 10→11, label 9→10.5, tick 8.5→10, note 7.5→8.5, kicker 8→9. |
| **Geometry** | LANDED. `SHAPES` vocabulary (wide/standard/tall/square/large); `FIGSIZE` is now a default, not a constraint. DPI floor, tight bbox and `finalize`-owned chrome all retained. |
| **Lint** | No width assertion existed to remove. `test_chrome_lint.py` is unchanged and still enforces chrome ownership. |
| **CVD** | Re-run, advisory. Passes at every severity; worst pair `warning|inert_fill` at ΔE 24.1 (deuteranope) / 24.9 (protanope), both above the 20.0 floor. |

**The type change was larger than a stack swap.** `apply()` had never set `font.family` at
all — chrome text passed the stack per call while *axis ticks and labels* rendered in
matplotlib's default face. Two faces per figure, unnoticed because both were quiet. The family
is now set globally, which is what makes the change visible.

Four collisions appeared at the new sizes and were fixed: the G25 base-rule annotation crossing
the min-5d curve, G26's "coin flip" label sitting on the mid-carry line, G26b's two end labels
converging on each other at zero, and G27's legend landing on the 2021–2026 row.
